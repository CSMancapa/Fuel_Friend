Petrol: shopping around won't help

Government fixes petrol prices for each area, so every station near you charges the same. The ways to pay less are to time your fill-up around price changes and to use a rewards card (step 4).

Diesel: the station you pick matters

Is the cheaper station worth the drive?

A cheaper station can cost more once you count the extra kilometres to reach it. Check before you go.

Use the rewards card your station accepts

Most brands give fuel rewards through a bank or retail loyalty programme. If you already have one of these, fill up at a brand that accepts it. Earn rates differ by programme and tier, so check yours.
